3) CONCRETE ISSUE FOUND
================================================================

The current DocumentManageRepository query has a broken q predicate:

@org.springframework.data.jpa.repository.Query("""
SELECT DISTINCT d
FROM DocumentManage d
LEFT JOIN PermissionManager p ON p.document = d
WHERE (d.owner.userId = :userId OR (p.user.userId = :userId AND p.accessType IN ('Owner','Editor','Viewer')))
  AND (:folderId IS NULL OR d.folder.folderId = :folderId)
  AND (:q LIKE LOWER(CONCAT('%', :q, '%')))
""")

Problem:
- the last clause compares the q parameter to itself
- it does not filter on d.title
- it is logically wrong even if it may not throw immediately

Correct form:

AND (:q IS NULL OR LOWER(d.title) LIKE LOWER(CONCAT('%', :q, '%')))

That is the correct title-search condition.
